# The mirror moat (2026-06-11)

A week of attribution legs versus our own stack produced two kinds of
verdicts: mechanisms that dominate the mirror (veto, reactive floor —
shipped, worked live), and everything else reading as either coin flips
or catastrophes. Today's triple refutation (h22/h16/adaptive-K all losing
50-90% paired to the incumbent) completes the pattern: the stack looks
like a sharp local optimum FROM INSIDE ITSELF.

But self-play margins measure divergence-from-the-incumbent, not ladder
strength. A 1-ship difference at step 1 cascades chaotically; the
incumbent's co-tuned mechanisms punish any calibration shift; symmetric
chaos yields 50%. The ladder above us is NOT our stack — fast expanders
(del Toro), originals (ledger-likes), unknown 1400-1700 styles. What
beats them is what buys rating, and we never measure against them.

Fresh frame: judge candidates by DELTA vs a diverse referee panel
(v7_0, ledger, champion, producer) — candidate-minus-baseline win/margin
per referee — instead of head-to-head vs self. Re-judge the shelved
"variance amplifiers" (expansion lane, commitment tax) under this frame
first: a 10-10-vs-self mechanism that wins +15% vs the panel is a ladder
winner the moat vetoed.
