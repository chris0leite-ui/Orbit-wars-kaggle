# The three shelved levers were not table-confounded — they're just parity (2026-06-02 PM)

The morning conclusion was "the kinematic table was the confound" — re-enabling it
is a real ~20 μ lever, and most prior strategy A/Bs were judged table-OFF or on the
singleton-corrupted harness, so three shelved levers (team-up, opening-planner,
position-score) deserved a re-test on the clean table-ON champion.

They got the re-test. **All three are parity-or-negative on the current champion, and
their stack is exactly 50/50.** So the table-confound did NOT hide a winner — it just
hid that these levers were never winners. Two distinct facts that the morning framing
ran together:
1. The table itself is a real lever (true).
2. Therefore the levers tested without it might be winners (false — re-tested, NULL).

The strongest evidence is the stack: all 8 CRN seed-pairs split W/L by seat. When the
seat decides every game, the two agents are the same strength. That's about as clean a
"no effect" as this harness produces.

## What this means going forward
The champion's design space along the proposer-coalition, opening-planner, and
value-head axes looks **saturated** — three independent levers, all parity. That's a
Rule-37 signal that the *value/coalition* family is tapped out for now; lift, if it
exists, is more likely on the conversion axis (we out-launch but under-capture) or a
genuinely different family — not in re-weighting what the chooser already does.
