The PI's modeling-failure hypothesis (strict-idle opp baseline +
`pv_horizon` mismatch) still fits — but the manifestation in real games
is "low mid-game launch rate," not "missed opening grab." Same root

### The pattern across all 4 games

Top-tier opponents **ramp launch rate as their planet count grows**:
3–5/window in opening → 19–25/window by mid-game. Our agent stays
**flat at ~8–13/window even as our planet count grows**. The chooser
doesn't scale emissions with capacity.

Mechanics behind our flat emission rate (verified in
`agents/v8_scavenge/main.py:700-795`):

- **Greedy non-dogpile emit (`:779-794`)** allows multiple moves per
  turn (one per src + one per tgt). With 10 planets this could be
  10 launches/turn — NOT a hard cap. Empirically we're emitting
  fewer than 1/turn on average mid-game; the cap isn't here.
- **Wallclock-bounded validate cap (`:758`)** —
  `effective_cap = min(N_VALIDATE, n_affordable)`. With 90-ms
  baseline + ~90 ms per-candidate validate within a 600-ms budget,
  `n_affordable ≈ 5-6` candidates per turn. So we score only the
  top-cheap-prerank 5-6 of ~80 (src, tgt) pairs.
- **`if delta > 0` emit gate (`:772`)** — filters survivors. With
  strict-idle baseline, most validated candidates have Δ ≤ 0
  because the baseline already says "I'm doing great by holding."
- **Strict-idle opp baseline** is the load-bearing failure: baseline
  favor grows turn-over-turn (we get bigger), so even a +production
  candidate has to BEAT that growing baseline — most don't.

Net: the 5-6 validates we run per turn mostly score Δ ≤ 0 because
the baseline lies. Fix the baseline (opp-trajectory + CRN), more
candidates pass Δ > 0, more launches emit per turn, launch rate
scales with our planet count.

## Defect classification

Per the methodology in §2 of the plan (`/root/.claude/plans/i-want-you-
to-misty-bubble.md`), the trace symptoms map to:

| Symptom | Defective component | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Idled when opp expanded | strict-idle baseline (`_build_idle_baseline`, main.py:514) | All 4 games: opp 137/74/105/113 vs us 41/52/93/51 |
| Captured then lost (mid-game collapse) | `_favor` F1/F2 balance + reinforce cadence | Felipe step 100→125: 8→0 planets; Naoism step 100→125: 6→2 planets |
| Right move available but not picked | `if delta > 0` emit gate | All games show flat launch rate ≤13 while we have 10+ planets |

All three failures route back to two model defects:

1. **Strict-idle opp baseline** — the K=30 idle rollout doesn't see
   opp's expanding launch rate. As game progresses, the baseline-vs-
   reality gap widens, and the chooser's "I'm fine, hold" verdict
   becomes increasingly wrong.
2. **`pv_horizon` over-weight on long-game F2** — `(my_prod − opp_prod) ×
   pv_horizon(step, 0, γ=0.99, t_total=500) ≈ ×99`. Median real game
   length is 171 (v8) / 206 (v9), so the production stream is
   integrated over a window that's 2–3× too long. Makes "I idle and
   keep my prod" look better than reality, because in reality the
   captured planet only produces for 100 more turns before opp
   recaptures.

These are the SAME two defects the session-start state-function-
principled-fix thought identified — confirmed across 4 games on top
of the original Felipe forensic. The reframing is "this hits mid-game
launch rate, not just opening cadence."

## Reinforce cadence — is Fix M2 closing the mid-economy gap?

v8 was supposed to address mid-game elimination via Fix M2 (reinforce
candidates for threatened own planets, commit 82b5526, audit
`2026-05-16-v8-scavenge-results.md`). Local panel jumped 37.5 % →
71.9 %. But live data shows it's not enough:

- v8 mid_economy_lost: 84 % of losses
- Felipe step-75→100 collapse: 12 → 8 → 6 → 2 → 0 planets across
  consecutive 25-step windows
- Naoism step-100→125: 6 → 2 planets

The reinforce candidates ARE enumerated (own threatened planets via
`WorldModel.time_to_enemy_threat`). But they have to clear the same
`if delta > 0` gate as offensive candidates. Under strict-idle
baseline:
- Baseline assumes opp idles → my threatened planet doesn't actually
  get attacked in the baseline rollout
- Reinforce candidate adds ships to my planet → Δ small or zero
  (just moving my own ships around)
- Gate fails → no reinforce emit
- Opp's actual wave lands next few turns → planet falls

So M2 isn't broken; it's **starved of the right signal** by the same
strict-idle baseline that causes opening + mid-game under-emission.
A correct opp_traj would predict opp's incoming wave in the baseline,
making the reinforce candidate's Δ correctly positive.

NOT a separate third defect. Same root cause as the launch-rate
ceiling, different downstream symptom.

| metric | v8 (52684059) | v9 (52687411) |

| games | 66 | 60 |

| median game length | 171 | 206 |

v9's 4-fix stack (reinforce-cheap-score, wider-threat, PV-discount,
4P sum-of-opps) **roughly doubled 4P winrate** and **extended median

opening-lost share rose 16 → 43, suggesting v9 trades early
aggression that wins games (e.g., snipe captures via the cheap
pre-rank reinforce-positivity) for some opening losses.

Net: v9 is the better submission and is currently on the ladder
(rolling-last-2 = [v9 1085, v8 1043]). Any refactor should be
A/B-tested against v9, not v8.
