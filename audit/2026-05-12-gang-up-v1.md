# Gang-up v1 — failed; substrate gap identified

> Branch `claude/optimize-ship-strategy-tDPXx`. The next architectural
> swing after the airtime/endgame/filter trio that all tied or regressed
> at 64-seed (`audit/2026-05-11-v3.5-airtime-and-endgame-burn.md`).

## Hypothesis

Phase-0 idle-source decomposition pointed at `intent.ships > src.ships`
in validate + arrival_size (~96% of all idle classifications). The
combat rule (`lib/combat.py::resolve_arrivals`) confirms same-owner
same-step arrivals SUM ships before resolution, so two small sources
arriving simultaneously CAN cover a target neither alone could. The
`gang_up_size` mechanism (new in `lib/mechanism.py`) was designed to
coordinate this: anchor on the slowest source's eta, allocate
proportional shares capped at `src.ships - GANG_UP_RESERVE`,
throttle faster sources DOWN so all gang members arrive simultaneously.

## A/B verdict — REGRESSION

64-seed 4-variant tournament vs baseline:

| Variant         | GANG_UP_ENABLED | TOLERANCE | RESERVE | winrate vs baseline | Wilson95 |
|-----------------|-----------------|-----------|---------|---------------------|----------|
| baseline        | 0 | 0 | 0 | (reference) | — |
| gangup_v1       | 1 | 0 | 0 | **43.8%** (56/72/0) | [35.5%, 52.4%] |
| gangup_loose    | 1 | 1 | 0 | **45.3%** (58/70/0) | [37.0%, 53.9%] |
| gangup_reserve  | 1 | 0 | 3 | **43.8%** (56/72/0) | [35.5%, 52.4%] |

All three gang-up variants regress at 128 games each. Wilson upper
bounds barely cross 50% — the regression is real, not noise.

## Falsifier: only PARTIALLY refuted

Phase-0 bucket counts on `gangup_v1` self-play (4 × 2P games, seeds 42-45)
vs `v3.4 baseline` (same seeds, `audit/2026-05-11-idle-breakdown-v3-snipe-phase0.md`):

|                          | baseline 2P | gangup_v1 2P | Δ |
|--------------------------|------------:|-------------:|--:|
| turns                    | 1,686 | 1,656 | — |
| `MECHANISM_DROP:validate`   | 9,665 | **5,861** | **−39%** |
| `MECHANISM_DROP:arrival_size` | 9,932 | **12,986** | **+31%** |
| total MECHANISM_DROP     | 19,597 | 19,181 | **−2%** |
| idle/turn                | 11.80 | 11.58 | −2% |

Gang-up successfully prevents `validate` from dropping unaffordable
intents (the throttled shares fit `src.ships`), but `arrival_size`
silently undoes the throttling: `intent.ships = max(intent.ships,
needed)` re-inflates each share to the full target garrison, then drops
when the re-inflated value exceeds `src.ships`. Net total drops fall
just 2% — the captures we *thought* we were enabling never materialise.

So the falsifier diagnosis "mechanically works but strategically
irrelevant" is **half right**: gang-up isn't even mechanically working
end-to-end. The substrate gap is that `gang_up_size` and `arrival_size`
don't share state. Until `arrival_size` knows about same-target sibling
intents, throttled shares get re-inflated and dropped.

## The real substrate gap

`gang_up_size` ran BEFORE `validate`, which lets unaffordable
single-source intents survive. But it ran BEFORE `arrival_size` too,
and `arrival_size` is the OTHER half of the single-source bump pattern
(`needed = target.ships + production*eta + 1`). For gang-up to actually
free captures:

1. **Option A — Make `arrival_size` group-aware.** Track sum of
   sibling intents at the same target; needed_per_intent =
   `(target_needed - sibling_sum)`. Requires arrival_size to iterate
   over the full intent list, not one at a time.
2. **Option B — Mark gang-up'd intents.** `gang_up_size` sets
   `intent.note = "gang_up"`; `arrival_size` skips the `max(intent.ships,
   needed)` bump for those. Cleaner but couples the two mechanisms via
   string sentinel.
3. **Option C — Pass an explicit pending ledger.** Refactor the
   mechanism signature to accept a shared per-turn state dict.
   Higher refactor cost; benefits future gang-up evolutions.

## Decision

**Revert `GANG_UP_ENABLED = 0` (default OFF).** Keep `gang_up_size`
in code as the v1 scaffolding for a future Option A redesign.

Move on to a non-mechanism axis (the plan's "re-examine target
selection in `settle_plan`, not mechanisms" guidance):
- **Threat-aware target deselection**: don't launch a source under
  incoming-enemy threat; hold for defense.
- **Predicted-needed affordability filter** (richer than v3.5's
  base_ships-only): compute the post-arrival_size needed at proposal
  time, filter on that.
- **Hungarian global allocation** (deferred from plan): explicit
  bipartite assignment of sources to targets, factoring in cumulative
  garrison requirements.
