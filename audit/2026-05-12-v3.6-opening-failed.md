## What was diagnosed

**The aggressive sizing in v3.5.1 (`lib/missions/snipe.py:208`) is gated
on `src.ships > AGGRESSIVE_MIN_GARRISON = 12`.** Home planets start with
10 ships. So at steps 0-2, the aggressive branch never fires; v3.5.1
falls through to `base_ships = max(1, t.ships + 1)`.

Empirical step-0 trace, real env config:

| Agent          | First-turn launches      | Ships sent |
| -------------- | ------------------------ | ---------- |
| v3.5.1         | 1 launch toward neutral  | 7 (target had 6 ships → target_min=7) |
| v3.6_opening   | 1 launch toward neutral  | 8 (src 10 − OPENING_RESERVE 2)        |

The numerical difference is +1 ship at step 0. Top-10 fingerprint claims
this should be **+14** at step 4 (bowwowforeach mean garrison-at-launch
7.7, with built-up production). My step-0 implementation captured the
wrong rung of the bowwow archetype.

## Why this failed (hypothesis)

bowwowforeach's "garrison-at-launch ≈ 7.7" is a measurement made at
**step 4** when the source has built up to ~20+ ships via production.
Sending ~14 ships leaves 8 garrison. That's a SIZE-matched fleet
launched after waiting for production to compound.

My implementation fires at step 0 with the starting 10-ship garrison
and sends 8 ships (drains to 2). That's:
- **Smaller fleet** than bowwow's 14
- **Earlier** than bowwow's step 4
- **Less defended source** (2 ships vs bowwow's 7.7) so we lose homes
  to opponents who arrive in the first 10-20 steps

The implementation captured one observable (garrison-at-launch) but
missed the binding observable (mean fleet size 38; p95 135). Top-10
sends BIG fleets late, not small fleets early.
