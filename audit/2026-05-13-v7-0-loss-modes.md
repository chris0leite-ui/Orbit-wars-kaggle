## Within `opening_lost`: what triggered?

- Launched late (first launch > step 6): **16 of 34 (47 %)**.
- Behind in planet count at step 30 AND ship-delta < −10: **25 of 34 (74 %)**.
- Both signals together: 7 games.

Median first-launch step across all 50 losers = **4** (matches top-10's
median). The OR-criterion means most opening losses are **not** "we
launched too late" — they're "we launched but didn't grab enough
planets fast enough." H11's mechanism is exactly that: fire a Mission
from EVERY owned planet with ships > 8 in the opening window,
front-loading neutral capture across all home planets in parallel.

## Strategic verdict

1. **H11 (opening grab) is the highest-EV cheap win.** 68 % of losses
   touch the opening; 4P games are 90 % opening-determined. The
   `lib/missions/opening.py` proposer is fully built and now wired into
   `_build_incumbent_intents` (this session). It fires from every
   owned planet with ships > 8 at steps 0–5, score
   `(remaining)^1.5 / (d + 1)` — directly targets the planet-count
   deficit at step 30.

2. **H15 (comet reject) is orthogonal** — addresses the "wasted launch
   slot" mode that doesn't appear in the bucket table because departing
   comets cost us per-source slots rather than producing visible
   tactical losses. Still cheap, still ships.

3. **Mid-economy bucket (16 games, mostly 2P) is the residual.** Likely
   addressed by depth-2 search (the C-track work) — the v7_0 chooser's
   "opp plays incumbent" assumption breaks mid-game when opponents
   adapt, and 14.8 % of moves disagree between v7_0 and the depth-2
   maximin oracle (`audit/2026-05-13-depth2-oracle-32seeds.csv`).

4. **Endgame drift never fires.** When we're ahead at step 400, we
   close. The PI's earlier concern about "endgame burn" via the
   `ENDGAME_NEUTRAL_BONUS` is empirically a non-issue at this ladder
   tier.

5. **Tactical missizing never fires.** We launch at roughly the same
   total volume as winners. The aggressive sizing layer (0.7 fraction)
   is calibrated correctly.
