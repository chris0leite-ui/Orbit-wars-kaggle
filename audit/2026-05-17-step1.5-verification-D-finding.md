call-site migration of the trajectory layer.
**Verification step D:** `python -m scripts.measure_hypothesis 52744856 --pull`
**Sample:** 45 episodes, 5652 fleets (sub 52744856, composite + A2
hybrid baseline, the rolling-last-2 champion as of 2026-05-17 PM).

## Headline finding

**Our live `sun_clip_rate` is 0.002 (0.2%).** The plan's premise was
that we sat near the 6%+ midpack floor; the Phase 7 migration of
`mechanism.sun_avoid → SunFilter` was titled "the 6%-savings PR."
**That premise is wrong by ~30x.** We are already far below top-10
(0.062) on sun discipline.

The trajectory layer is still valuable, but its `sun_clip_rate`
justification is dead.

## What this overturns in the plan

The plan's Phase 7 migration sequence ordered `mechanism.sun_avoid →
SunFilter` as the headline (`§ Migration sequence 5.2.1`, "THE
6%-savings PR"). With the actual sun-clip rate at 0.2%, that
migration produces ~0 μ. The trajectory layer's justification
shifts from:

  ~~closes the 6% sun-clip gap to top-10~~

to:

  1. **Efficiency** — eliminates per-call `World+WorldModel` rebuilds
     so the chooser can score more candidates per turn budget. The
     **multi_launch_turn_rate gap (−19 pp)** is bounded by candidate
     evaluation cost today; making evaluation cheap is the lever.
  2. **Sparse hypothetical-launch overlay** — Phase 3 enables
     simultaneously evaluating N parallel launch candidates in O(N)
     per-overlay cost. Directly attacks the gang-up gap.
  3. **Substrate for Steps 2-4** — shot validator labels are
     `World.ownership_at(target, eta+10)` queries; value head is a
     leaf-eval on the layer's lazy timeline; learned opp model
     overlays opponent hypothetical launches via the same overlay
     mechanism.

## The real gap is fleet aggression

| signal                  | us    | top-10 | direction |
|-------------------------|------:|-------:|-----------|
| active_turn_fraction    | 0.344 |  0.480 | launch more turns |
| multi_launch_turn_rate  | 0.289 |  0.480 | gang up when launching |
| comet_kill_rate         | 0.001 |  0.034 | engage comets |
| first_launch_step       |  4.87 |   4.10 | open faster |

A first-order interpretation: **we play safe, top-10 plays often**.
Our safety advantages (waste-attack matches v15; sun-clip,
trajectory-waste, comet-kill all at floor) come at the cost of
~40% fewer launches per game. The composite head's
`waste_penalty + capture_credit` formulation explicitly penalises
risky launches; the empirical winrate lift (+3 pp vs v15) suggests
the trade is net positive against MIDPACK opponents but
under-commits against top-10.

## Revised next moves

Given the finding, three reasonable trajectories:

### Option A — Keep building trajectory layer, retarget Phase 7
Continue Phase 7 but reorder: **`composite_capture_value` rewrite
to query `World` instead of rebuilding `WorldModel`** becomes the
headline. Measure: turn-budget freed → candidate count per turn →
multi_launch_turn_rate. Defer or skip the `sun_avoid → SunFilter`
swap (no μ to gain).

### Option B — Pause trajectory layer, attack aggression directly
The aggression gap might respond to cheaper interventions:
- Lower the composite head's `waste_penalty` coefficient
- Reduce `MIN_FLEET_SIZE` (currently 3); allow 2-ship probes
- Raise `MAX_FLEETS_PER_TURN` in the chooser to allow gang-up shots
- Force-launch on every turn while we have a 30-ship-strong source

Risk: Rule 40 (modeling-correctness over restriction-tuning) — these
are "easy fix bump-a-constant" interventions that may not generalise.
But cheap to try.

### Option C — Both in parallel
The trajectory layer keeps progressing as substrate; ALSO pre-register
a `multi_launch_turn_rate ≥ 0.40` aggression-tuning hypothesis as
the NEXT submit. Cheap aggression tweak ships first; trajectory
layer waits for Step 2-4 to find use.

PI judgement call. The strategic principle from CLAUDE.md Rule 40
prefers Option A (modeling fixes over constant bumps), but the
**immediate ladder cost** of Option A is highest (~3-5 more days
of layer work before a submit) vs Option B's same-day submit
window.
