## Why the patches failed

The replay-mining was correct about waste rates (15 % comet shots → 100 % miss; 60–70 % captures lost back within 50 turns). But fixing waste at the **explicit chooser-evaluation layer** double-counts signal that v15's **implicit rollout layer** already encodes:

- **v15's reactive-opp rollout** simulates the opp counter-recapture at every step; my captured planet flips back inside the rollout; my F2 leaf-favor drops; the candidate's Δ collapses naturally. The rollout *is* the lost-back filter.
- **v15's `_favor` PV-discount (γ=0.99)** already weighs late captures lower than early ones, so a fragile late capture is worth less even before flip.
- **E1** prefilter cuts captures whose local force ratio < 0.35 — but the rollout already finds these and de-prioritises them via the flip. E1 removes signal the rollout was correctly using.
- **E2** hold-check runs a longer rollout under counter-recapture-aware opp, then multiplies Δ by hold_p — but the rollout estimate is itself noisy, and multiplying compounds the noise.
- **Patch A** joint-emit's greedy commit picks highest-solo-Δ first, then rescores. The first commit's Δ was scored against me-idle; the second's against me-fires-first-commit. By the time the second rolls in, the first is already pushing F2 high in baseline → second commit's marginal Δ is small → on noise it sometimes commits unhelpful captures.

Net: v15 captures aggressively, sometimes loses some back, but wins by force of production. v21's three filters trim the aggression at three independent layers, all in the same direction. The combined trimming removes more value than the waste it prevents.

This is the **`explicit-rewrite-of-implicit-behavior`** friction (already in `audit/friction.md` from 2026-05-16, PR #25 lessons). It just fired again — promotion candidate.

## Lessons / friction promotion candidates

- **`local-overpredict-2x` re-fires at n=16.** Wilson CI of width 0.45 cannot distinguish parity from a 15 pp regression. n=32 minimum for any "does the change lift" decision. Promotion candidate: refuse to call `INCONCLUSIVE` n=16 results as evidence of parity.
- **`explicit-rewrite-of-implicit-behavior` re-fires.** Before adding an explicit filter / scoring layer on top of the chooser, identify where the existing rollout already encodes that signal. If the rollout encodes it, add the filter *inside* the rollout (stronger opp policy, longer horizon) rather than *on top of* it. Already in CLAUDE.md Rule 40 framing; promote to a concrete pre-flight check: "does the rollout's reactive-opp catch this? if yes, do not add a separate filter."
- **Diagnostic-sample-size over-fit.** The 4-seed diagnostic that pointed at "Patch A over-commits" was over-fit to seed 1002. n=4 per-game diagnostics need at least the same seed set as the h2h panel to be load-bearing.

## Pivot

New plan at `audit/2026-05-17-v21-pivot-plan.md`: stronger rollout opp model (counter-recapture-aware lite_greedy applied to the REGULAR rollout, not just to E2's hold-check). Targets the same lost-back failure mode but from inside the layer where v15's leaf F2 actually consumes the signal — the right place per the lesson above.
