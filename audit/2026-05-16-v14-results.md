## Wallclock fix (intra-session)

Initial 4P bench showed max=1094ms (2/80 turns ≥1000ms). Root cause:
`t_deadline` was anchored at the *post-pre-rank* timestamp, so 4P
pre-rank (~250ms with 3 opps) effectively granted the maximin stage
the full `WALLCLOCK_BUDGET_MS=600ms` *on top*, totaling
250+600+200ms-in-flight = 1050ms+.

Fix at commit `73787cd`: capture `t_agent_start` at agent() entry,
set the maximin hard cap to `t_agent_start + (wallclock_budget −
safety_ms)` where `safety_ms = 200ms` (2P) / `300ms` (4P) accommodates
one in-flight `_score_action`. Re-test on 8 games: max=834ms, zero
≥1000ms.

## Why panel and 4P-vs-v7_0 are misleading

This is the third architecture (v13 hybrid, v14 maximin) showing the
same gap between panel/4P-vs-v7_0 and head-to-head-vs-v12:

| Metric | v12 | v13 | v14 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2P panel vs v3.5.1 | 75% | 94% | (unrun) |
| 2P panel vs v7_0 | 81% | 75% | (unrun) |
| 4P vs 3× v7_0 | (unrun) | 13/20 (65%) | 16/20 (80%) |
| **2P vs v12** | (self) | 47% (n=32) | **50% (n=64)** |
| **4P vs 3× v12** | (self) | 35% (n=20) | **25% (n=20)** |

**Diagnosis**: every chooser variant we build on the v9 base
"discovers" the same set of high-value moves and avoids the same set
of pitfalls. When you put two of these variants head-to-head, neither
finds an exploitable difference — they make similar choices at
similar quality. The fancy modeling layers (top_tier hybrid, archetype
maximin) help against opponents whose moves DIFFER from the family's
default (v3.5.1, weak v7_0), but they don't help against opponents
who already make the same kinds of moves we do.

This is the **chooser-family saturation** hypothesis materializing
hard. The ceiling is the leaf scorer (`_favor` = F1 + F2_pv_discount)
+ the `fast_sim` simulator + the candidate enumeration — all shared.

## Recommendation

**HOLD v14 submission.** Submitting evicts v9 (μ=1123) from rolling-
last-2 for an expected μ ≈ v12 = 1119 — no expected gain.

## What we proved with v14

Positive:
- The minmax/maximin framing **can be made cheap** (~120ms p95 in 2P,
  ~370ms in 4P) — v7_3's wallclock concern is solvable
- The maximin stage **doesn't regress wallclock** vs v12 when properly
  budget-guarded
- v9's leaf scorer + archetype maximin is **structurally sound** —
  it doesn't crash, it produces sensible picks
- 4P-vs-v7_0 winrate is the best we've measured (80%)

Negative:
- Maximin doesn't beat the existing chooser family in head-to-head
- Panel signal continues to mislead (same lesson as v13)

## Next paths

Forward options ordered by expected lift:

1. **Different chooser family (highest expected lift, most work).**
   - v4_planner family with v9's 4-fix stack + opp_traj
   - MCTS / minimax at depth=2 with v9's leaf scorer
   - Receding-horizon planner with learned action sequencing
2. **Learned value head (longest build, biggest ceiling).** Train a
   small neural model on self-play game outcomes; replace `_favor`
   for leaf scoring. The hand-tuned F1+F2 is the saturation surface.
3. **Direct ladder-loss replay-pull** (cheapest, most empirical).
   Pull 10-20 v12 ladder losses, classify by phase, target the
   dominant failure mode. Same methodology that produced v9's 4-fix
   stack. Likely faster path to +30-50μ than another architecture.
